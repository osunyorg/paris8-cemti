It was the concluding event of the research project "PERFORM - Performing Provenance: Contemporary Art, Digital Spaces, and Things from the Cameroon Grasslands"

text: >-

Venue: Rautenstrauch-Joest-Museum, Cäcilienstraße 29-33, 50667 Cologne

Scientific organisation: Prof. Dr. Ursula Frohne, Alexandra Galitzine-Loumpet, PhD, Julie Peghini, Jolanda Saal-Basar, Nanette Snoep

Funded by the Federal Government Commissioner for Culture and the Media and the French Ministry of Culture.
